Résumé of the workspace review

This page stands in for the converted copy of a document in the demo. Every document of every workspace previews as this page, served from the same origin as the demo so that the text can be searched from the preview panel.

1. What a workspace is

A workspace is a set of documents that can be reused. A workspace is shared with owners and readers; owners manage its access, readers can only look. Searching the workspace for a word finds it in the names, and this box finds it in the text.

2. Accents and case

The search ignores case and accents: “resume”, “RESUME” and “Résumé” are the same word; so are “cafe” and “café”, “naive” and “naïve”. Try them one after the other, and use Enter and Shift+Enter to move between the occurrences.

3. Uploads

Documents are uploaded to a workspace in chunks and then indexed. While a document is uploading or indexing its card says so; once it is indexed its date is shown instead. A workspace that has nothing in it yet says so too.

4. Long texts
