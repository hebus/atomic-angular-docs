# Buttons (/docs/galactik/buttons)

The button family — Button and the Toggle/ToggleGroup segmented control — and the variants they share.



A directive that restyles the native `<button>` element, and a pressed/unpressed pill that works standalone
or grouped into an exclusive or multi-selection toolbar.
