# Display (/docs/galactik/display)

Presentation primitives — Tag, Badge, Bullet, Kbd, Avatar, List, RowGrid — with no business logic of their own.



Small, composable primitives for showing content — a status, a category, an identity, a row of selectable or
navigable items. None of these depend on a service or a store; they render whatever inputs and projected
content you give them.
