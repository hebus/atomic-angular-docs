# Collections & labels (/docs/atomic-angular/collections-and-labels)

Grouping documents into a user-managed collection, and tagging them with shared or personal labels.



Two independent ways a user organizes documents outside of search itself: grouping them into a named
collection (a *basket*), and tagging individual documents with public or private labels.
