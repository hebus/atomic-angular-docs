# Bookmarks & saved searches (/docs/atomic-angular/bookmarks-and-searches)

Bookmarking individual documents, saving or recalling whole searches, and acting on a multi-document selection.



Three independent ways a user keeps track of things across sessions: a single bookmarked document, a whole
saved (or automatically recorded) search, and — for the moment several documents are selected at once — a
floating action bar.
